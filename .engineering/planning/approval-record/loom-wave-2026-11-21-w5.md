three stories on 0.86.0 and the move alone in the wave after.

(`.engineering/drafts/ess096-probe-run.md`, managed tree `loom-ess096-probe`), revised, and
approved by the plan critic panel in its second round
(`review-result:ess-096-upgrade-acceptance-1` and `-2`, `review-result:ess-096-upgrade-design-1`
and `-2`). Its two specification decisions were asked of session `sdc` with defaults, taken on its
silence and confirmed by it in the words above. It moved to `proposed` under
`approval-record:sdc-requests-accepted-by-default-2026-11-21`.
