## Question

Is wave 2026-10-12-w1 (story:ess-059-upgrade, then story:granted-gate-ends-run) approved as one
wave, and which rule does the approval gate follow after a step performed under an authority grant?

## Why it is open

story:granted-gate-ends-run changes when `run_until_blocked` ends `AwaitingApproval`, which a
caller matching that outcome sees. Its options:

- A: a step whose request needed authority, was granted and was performed passes the gate; every
  other step reads it as today (the ungated case of `runtime_effect.rs:1047` keeps ending
  `AwaitingApproval`). The plugin host's re-run rule is removed.
- B: keep the gate as it is and record that in a design note; the plugin host keeps its re-run
  rule.
- C: leave out of the awaited actions every action granted in this Run, as a denied one is left
  out today. On `inbound-answer@1` the Run still ends awaiting `reply.propose`, so the re-run rule
  stays.

Recommended: A.

## Decision (2026-10-12)

Approved as one wave, in the order ess-059-upgrade, granted-gate-ends-run, with one integration
branch, one pull request and an adversary pass on granted-gate-ends-run. The approval gate follows
option A: a step whose request needed authority, was granted by the provider and was performed by
the effect port passes the gate; every other step reads it as before, so the ungated case of
`runtime_effect.rs:1047` still ends `AwaitingApproval`. The plugin host's re-run rule is removed,
and the CHANGELOG names the change for callers that match `AwaitingApproval` after a granted
effect. After the merge on green CI, release 0.16.0. Builds run one at a time, only with 28G or
more free on `/`, each tree's cache discarded before the next build starts.
