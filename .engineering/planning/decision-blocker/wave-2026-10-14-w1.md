## Units

U1 story:change-evidence-order: the slice submits `implementation_change`, a bugfix's
`regression_test_result`, `observed_at` and the trusted `producer`, and its governor evaluates at
the host instant, so `red_first.required` can be discharged. ESS first: `intake.routing` declares
the slice's evidence. Surfaces: `crates/loom-intake-slice`, `crates/loom-governor/src/lib.rs`,
`crates/loom-cli`, `ess/intake/`, `generated/rust/intake/`. One adversary pass: it changes what a
governed bugfix admits as done.

U2 story:mandate-authority: a new crate `b10x-loom-mandate` answers Commission's authority port
with Mandate v0.9.0. ESS first: a `loom.authority` domain. Surfaces: `crates/loom-mandate`,
`ess/domains/authority.yaml`, `generated/rust/loom/`, `Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock`, the Commission
dependency guard. One adversary pass: it is an authority provider.

The two share only the documents every unit updates (AGENTS.md, README, CHANGELOG,
`website/data/status.json`, the generated crate page); they run in parallel trees and merge one
after the other, the second regenerating `website/docs/reference/crates.md`.

Not in the wave: story:mandate-approval-on-resume, which waits on
decision-blocker:mandate-approval-recording; the drafts that wait on an ESS release, Connectors or
Substrate.
