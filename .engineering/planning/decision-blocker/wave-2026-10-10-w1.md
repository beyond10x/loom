## Question

Is wave 2026-10-10-w1 (story:ess-057-upgrade, story:fallback-selection-recording,
story:selection-telemetry) approved as one wave, and with which details for the recording of a
confidence fallback?

## Why it is open

All three stories edit `ess/domains/run.yaml` and run one after another; the recording of a fallback
changes what a caller sees in Loom's record (two selections where it saw one `Hybrid`).

## Decision (2026-10-10)

Approved as one wave, in the order ess-057-upgrade, fallback-selection-recording,
selection-telemetry, with one integration branch, one pull request and an adversary pass on
fallback-selection-recording. The details in story:fallback-selection-recording's body hold: each
selection records its own selector's strategy, a provided `ActionSelector` method carries the
overruled pick, the replacement's existence is checked by the behaviour, and the `Selections` view
publishes `replaced_by`. After the merge on green CI, a minor release whose CHANGELOG names the
changed record of a selection on a fallback. Builds run one target at a time, each unit tree
finished with its cache discarded before the next starts.
