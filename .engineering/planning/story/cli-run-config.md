## Outcome

`b10x-loom run --config loom.yaml` runs on the models its configuration names, through
`loom_sdk::models`: the agent and the classifier are the routes of the roles `agent` and
`classifier`, credentials resolved from the routes document's `secret:` references, so a route
whose account needs a credential is no longer refused as it is under `--catalog`
(`crates/loom-cli/src/model_catalog.rs:111`). `--model` and `--classifier-model`, when given,
override one role each with a name of the same routes document. Both models are settled before any
model call.

## Acceptance

In `crates/loom-cli/tests/run_config.rs`:
- `run_config_uses_the_routes_of_each_role`: against a local stub endpoint declared in a routes
  document with a bearer `env:` secret, a `run --config` sends the classifier's turn to the
  classifier's route and the agent's turns to the agent's, each with the bearer from the variable.
- `a_flag_overrides_one_role`: `--model other` with `--config` sends the agent's turns to `other`
  and keeps the classifier's route.
- `a_role_neither_configured_nor_given_is_refused_before_any_model_call`: a config that declares no
  `classifier` and no `--classifier-model` exits 1 (`run --help`: the run failed),
  its standard error names the role `classifier` and the flag `--classifier-model`, and the stub
  endpoint received zero requests.
- `config_and_catalog_together_are_refused` (clap conflict).
- A route whose model cannot take a forced tool call is refused before any model call, naming the
  role, as `--catalog` does.
- `run --help` documents `--config`; the `run --output jsonl` contract is unchanged
  (`task docs-check`).
