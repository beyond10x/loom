| 1 | `crates/loom-governor/src/lib.rs:1060` | The red case above: exit 101. The document says `computed_from.at` 12:00:01; the frontier was decided at 12:00:00 | `slice_governor` (`crates/loom-intake-slice/src/run.rs:171-177`) builds the governor for `b10x-loom run` and `resume` with `ClockTime(SystemClock)`, which reads the clock to the second on every evaluation (`clock.rs:98`). Any host whose two reads straddle a second gets the mismatch. The clock that ticks on each read is my stand-in; nothing calls the new method yet | NEEDS-CHANGE | introduced | blocker |

- **Cause:** `decision_document` runs a second evaluation through `decided`. Its doc comment says it returns "the decision `frontier` renders into its frontier id". That only holds when the time stands still and nothing is written between the two calls. The story's "Why" says the reverse: "not a second evaluation beside it".
- **Fix options (not applied):**
  - return the frontier and its document from one `decided` call;
  - or take the frontier id and refuse when the re-evaluated document's id differs;
  - or narrow the claim in the doc comment, the CHANGELOG and AGENTS.md.
