**5. Attacked and could not break**
- **Narrowing:**
  - A proposal of another step's action, of an action the frontier does not list, or of a blocked action is never returned (`workflow.rs:1387`).
  - Reads proposed together are never returned (`:1407`).
  - The narrowed frontier drops other actions' entries and their reasons (`:2079`). Claims and obligations are case-level and passed through.
- **Deterministic and unlisted steps:** a deterministic step never calls the executor. A step that is unlisted, narrowed out, or missing an input ends `NeedsHumanJudgment` before the host's executor is built.
- **Executor builder:** built once per call, with no position lock held (`:1378`). A panic propagates without poisoning any state.
- **Pass-through outcomes:** `Suspended`, `NeedsHumanJudgment`, `CaseMoved` and `CompletedLocalReasoning` pass through. The runtime treats `CompletedLocalReasoning` like `NoUsefulAction` (`runtime.rs:1357`).
- **Budget:**
  - It sums both requests of a call (control above).
  - It stops when the budget is reached, as the old loop did (`turn_loop/mod.rs:3010`).
  - The budget stop is checked before an unusable answer is reported (`:2130`).
- **Harness references:** `git grep -n 'harness::'` on workflow.rs and the two migrated adversary files prints nothing.
- **Migrated tests:** the two adversary files still hold 7 and 25 cases, and no case was weakened.
- **Not a finding:**
  - `WorkflowExecutor` is no longer `Send`/`Sync`, but the type is not on the SDK stability page.
  - The chooser cannot be called inside a Tokio runtime, but the production HTTP transport already had that limit.
- **Not verified:** `a_supplied_executors_proposal_the_frontier_blocks_is_never_proposed` would likely stay green if the frontier half of the check at `:1387` were removed, because Commission refuses a blocked action anyway. I did not run that change: building a copy needs a target directory outside the tree, which the rules forbid.
