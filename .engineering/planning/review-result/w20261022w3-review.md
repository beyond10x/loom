## Findings

- tool-decisions (fixed in 027f98be): a b10x observe session reported `Some([])` instead of unknown
  (blocker); the census split and the decider counts were not compared with the record; an empty
  tool name was accepted.
- agent-domain (fixed in 37404333): `bearer_env` accepted a value that is no variable name; blank
  text was accepted; a custom YAML tag was accepted; ports outside 1–65535 were accepted; a deeply
  nested flow document took 68.6 s to refuse (now 0.23 s, bounded by `MAX_FLOW_OPENERS`); the
  primitive types a spec holds were not reachable through `loom_sdk::agent`.
- runtime-events (fixed in 11068f7c): a resume whose case moved after its take emitted `StepStarted`
  for an invocation that never happened. The reviewer also restored a frontier assertion the
  implementor had weakened after the red run, as
  `security_step_started_names_the_frontier_each_executor_call_was_handed`, which passes.

  sink leaves a performed effect unobserved; the specification allows it ("a panicking sink is the
  host's").
- `models::parse_config` has no `MAX_FLOW_OPENERS` bound; only `loom_sdk::agent::parse` has.
