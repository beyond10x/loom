Cases are in `crates/loom-session-executor/tests/security_w20261030w3_c.rs`.
- Green: `a_directory_of_another_user_at_the_path_is_refused_and_left_as_it_is`.
- Red: `a_root_swapped_for_a_link_while_its_leftover_is_emptied_reaches_nothing_outside_it`. Setup: a leftover root with 20,000 entries, in a scratch of mode 0777 with no sticky bit. The root is swapped for a link with an atomic renameat2(RENAME_EXCHANGE) during the emptying. Result: 19,999 of the user's files at the link's target were removed, `copy` and `home` were made there, and the session ran there.

The default sticky `/tmp` stops other users. The case is reached only through a scratch that others can write and that lacks the sticky bit, or through a process of the same user.

Reviewed and not faulted:
- another user's directory at the path
- a pre-created link or non-directory
- the emptying follows no link
- root mode 0700
- the lock and its denial-of-service bound
- the dev/inode re-check
- effective_uid
- name validation

Coordinator decision: fix it in correction round 1, even though the default scratch does not reach it. Before claim, refuse a scratch directory that a group or other users can write unless it has the sticky bit. Also refuse a non-directory or foreign entry after the `created` shortcut at lib.rs:3172.
