**5. Attacked and could not break**
- Re-invocation: same id and revision; authority asked again; revision re-read after an allow; deny remembered; a moved case is not invoked again.
- Idle count: a pending answer followed by `Performed`, an ordinary refusal, a final refusal, or a drop. A dropped repeated admission is never judged idle, which matches the spec and the module doc.
- Precedence: `Completed` is loaded before a budget stop after a performed effect. A kept refusal beats `Waiting`. A spending breach beats `EffectRefused`. Same-iteration step and run budgets give the `RunBudget` reason.
- Trial limits on a pending request and on pending fan-out reads, the approval gate around a pending request, and resume at budget 1.
- `connector.rs` passes `Pending` through (`:274`). Its "handed once" doc (`:11`) is stale, but no invoker answers `Pending`.
- The slice maps `Waiting` with a `RunBudget` reason to `StepBudget` while `Suspended(RunBudget)` is an error (`run.rs:940` vs `:877`). The slice sets no run budget, so this cannot happen there.
