**5. Attacked and could not break**
- Every bundled and plugin id maps to exactly its registered name.
- Every suggestion passes `parse_registration` and registers.
- Ids with several dots, leading or trailing dots or underscores, a hyphen already present, digits, `@0`, `@01`, no major, `@1@1`, or a length of 100 or 101 all behave as expected.
- Control and bidi characters are escaped in the refusal.
- `parse_registration` is equivalent to the old rule, so nothing that registered before is refused.
- No refusal echoes YAML text: the id is echoed only after it maps to `[a-z0-9._-]`.
